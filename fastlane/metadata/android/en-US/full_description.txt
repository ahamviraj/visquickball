Vi's Quick Ball adds a small floating control for Android navigation.

GESTURES
• Tap requests Back.
• Double-tap requests Home.
• Long press requests Recent Apps, where supported by the device.
• Drag the ball to move it; it docks to the nearest screen edge.

Quick Ball also offers an optional Android Quick Settings tile and a
home-screen widget.

SETUP
Grant Display over other apps access and enable the Quick Ball Accessibility
Service manually in Android Settings. The service performs navigation actions
in response to gestures and observes window-state metadata to hide the ball
over some system panels. It does not retrieve accessibility nodes or window
text. Android versions and device manufacturers may limit Recent Apps or
background behavior.
